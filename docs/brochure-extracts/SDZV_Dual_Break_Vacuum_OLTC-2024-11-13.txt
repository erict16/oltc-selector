Official name: 双断口真空有载分接开关 (combined).
Replaces single-break vacuum bottles (SHZV) with series dual-break
(V11/V12, V21/V22). Max rated step voltage 6000 V. III step capacity
to 4500 kVA. Y-connection three-phase; single-phase any connection.

Type example: SDZVIII-600Y/126C-10193W

No extra dual-break letter in the type string.
